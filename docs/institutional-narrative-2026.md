## Source and editorial decisions

The reference is `IRESEN_Narratif_Institutionnel.pdf`, 25 pages, 1,626,605 bytes,
SHA-256 `fc0634d62210a53800196d79f6a27af9ea76228717a4ea6519dfa3b424733fe6`.
The supplied PDF and its seven embedded diagrams were reviewed. The separately
linked ClickUp illustration was inaccessible; no independent comparison is claimed.
The PDF remains a private reference outside the repository, CMS and public search.
Its announced Annex A is absent from the supplied 25 pages.

The French mission on page 6 is reproduced verbatim on Home and Institute. The
shorter message in the page-9 graphic is an adapted headline, not a replacement
for the official mission. EN/AR are complete working adaptations for owner review;
the source provides no approved translations. The English signature is not a
commission to replace the existing global slogan.

The implemented hierarchy follows pages 9–15:

- **Research & Innovation is the core activity**, with Develop, Test and Valorise.
- **Studies & Expertise, Skills & Capacities, Cooperation & Outreach** are three
  complementary dimensions, which can also answer needs directly.
- Eight shared capabilities are mobilised according to the need. This is neither
  an organisation chart nor a compulsory project procedure.
- The 2035 ambition has three orientations: excellence, technological mastery,
  value creation and impact. It is an ambition, not a current accreditation.

Funding, programmes and infrastructure are instruments of this model. A technology
developed elsewhere may be tested, an experiment may inform further research, and
knowledge or know-how may be useful without immediate industrial transfer.

## Messages and terminology

The five master messages remain distinct from the three missions and the three
complementary dimensions:

1. Develop knowledge and solutions.
2. Test technologies and demonstrate their potential.
3. Valorise research and prepare its application.
4. Inform choices and strengthen capabilities.
5. Bring partners together and amplify contributions.

The mission is exact source copy; brief introductions and audience messages are
adaptations. Use the [working glossary](narrative-glossary.md) for FR/EN/AR consistency.
The core data and keys live in `src/lib/institutional-narrative.ts` and `Narrative`
in the language catalogs. Existing original bibliographic and patent source fields
are preserved.
