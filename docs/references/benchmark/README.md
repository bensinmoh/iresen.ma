## What matters most for IRESEN

The central lesson is **explain, demonstrate, connect**: establish the visitor's
need and IRESEN's contribution, show relevant evidence, then make the next
interaction understandable. This reinforces the current three contribution
domains and their shared cooperation enabler; it supplies no new institutional
facts or measured outcomes.

The strongest editorial safeguard is the distinction between inputs, activities,
outputs and outcomes. Funding, infrastructure, project counts, publications and
patent filings cannot stand in for demonstrated adoption or external benefit.
Keep evidence close to its claim, with scope, period, source and precise partner
roles where these are available. Generated illustrations can explain a concept;
they cannot prove the existence or operation of a real IRESEN facility.

For information architecture, distinguish themes, axes, programmes, projects,
platforms, technology/IP assets and publications. They are related entities,
rather than successive levels of one hierarchy. Reuse trustworthy records and
contextual links instead of copying descriptions into multiple pages. This is
an editorial modelling principle, not a commission for new CMS collections,
filters, menus or routes.

For layout, choose a hierarchy for the content: a clear introduction, meaningful
facts or capabilities, useful media, evidence, related material and a relevant
next action. Vary reading blocks, rows, facts and collections when they serve
different purposes. Avoid repeated card grids and decorative sections without
new information. The suggested anatomy is adaptable, not a fixed section order,
hero treatment or universal page template. Existing IRESEN components, fonts,
colors, geometry and responsive rules remain authoritative.

| Page/content context            | Useful benchmark lens                                                                          | IRESEN boundary                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Institutional or expertise page | External need, contribution, capabilities and supporting examples before organisational detail | Preserve the current narrative and supplied claims; no invented proof                                                                              |
| Research theme or programme     | Plain-language scope and questions, then related work and capabilities                         | Themes, axes and programmes remain distinct; link only reachable approved destinations                                                             |
| Project or achievement          | Challenge, work, partner roles, dated status, result and evidence                              | Separate delivered outputs from expected or measured outcomes                                                                                      |

| Publications and resources      | Retrieval through consistent bibliographic metadata and contextual summaries                   | The 1,181 selected records for T4 2026 remain staged until section development is commissioned; preserve original titles/authors and dated metrics |
| Collaboration                   | Visitor need, suitable pathway and an honest explanation of the next step                      | Do not promise services, funding or access; contact retains its local email-draft workflow                                                         |

For each commissioned page, use a few relevant questions:

- Does the introduction explain the purpose and value to its intended visitor?
- Are claims supported, dated where relevant and distinguished from ambitions?
- Does the layout reveal essential information before specialist detail, with
  appropriate metadata and media for its content type?
- Do related links and the next action lead to real, suitable destinations?
- Does the content work in FR/EN/AR, mobile, RTL, keyboard and enlarged text?

Missing metadata or evidence should produce a specific content dependency, not
invented values or an elaborate empty library. Future public additions still
require approved localized [search references](../../search.md#adding-public-content)
and the relevant implementation/visual checks. This benchmark itself remains
excluded from the public index. Alliances, full Phase III drafting and final
editorial/translation approval retain their existing scope and deferrals.
