## What each document contributes

**N1 — Narrative framework.** “Sur quoi repose la nouvelle lecture proposée” and
“Ce que nous voulons faire comprendre sur IRESEN” organize the story around the
need, IRESEN's role, the contribution of partners and the path from research to
use. Funding and infrastructure are instruments within that story. Morocco's
energy transition, industrial performance and energy/water/resource needs provide
context; they do not establish measured results for individual projects.

The existing **Développer · Éprouver · Valoriser** framework is reinforced:
develop knowledge or responses; test performance and conditions of use; prepare
maturation, transfer and adoption. Research and experimentation can feed back
into each other. A useful outcome can be knowledge, a method, skills or a better
informed decision; commercialisation is not guaranteed or mandatory.

“Six capacités pour rendre ce parcours possible” identifies scientific and
technological expertise; programming and collaborative R&D&I; platforms and
experimentation; valorisation and transfer; human capabilities and expert
networks; partnerships, cooperation and resources. These are complementary
content dimensions, not six compulsory navigation branches or an organigram.

**N2 — Architecture of communications supports.** “Les questions que nos supports
doivent traiter” allocates the common story and evidence to the website, brochure
and institutional presentation. “Comment organiser le site” broadly follows the
existing brief's page boundaries, using short homepage summaries linked to
deeper pages. The indicative eight-slide presentation, eight-page brochure and
later one-pager/report/thematic notes are communications recommendations. They
do not commission those deliverables in this repository task. Final writing and
production belong to Phase III, as N2's introduction states.

N1 and N2 contain different central-message formulations. N1 also offers an
English mission statement and “Research that moves energy forward.” as a
signature. Preserve these as source variants; final copy selection, slogan
adoption and each institutional translation still require review.

**S — Detailed website design proposal.** “1 Arborescence du site,” the P01–P16
specifications and “4 Fiches de détail” translate the story into visitor goals,
candidate section sequences, layouts, relations and editorial requirements.
They provide useful starting points for page work after scope validation.
Section titles, module counts, card counts and layout ratios are suggestions.

## Evidence and publication implications

N1's “Comment lire les affirmations du nouveau narratif,” N2's “Les preuves rendent
les messages concrets” and S's “La rigueur des affirmations” consistently require
the following distinctions:

- A case explains the need, actions, precise IRESEN and partner contributions,
  verified result, maturity and known next step. Expected results and measured
  effects remain separate. A signed agreement alone proves neither funding nor
  transfer; a platform's existence does not prove open access.
- A figure needs a definition, unit, scope, period and verification/update date.
  Historical funding-instrument figures must not be conflated with the institute's
  wider portfolio. Internal evidence retains its source and confirming owner;
  only authorized proof enters public projections.
- Platforms and expertise describe actual operators, conditions and per-capacity
  availability. Patent applications, granted rights and active rights are distinct;
  transfer and adoption claims need their own evidence.
- The 2035 ambition and the proposed 2026–2035 convention are distinct from
  achievements and adopted commitments. Confirm the official name, legal identity,
  organisation and convention status before drafting public claims. Scientific
  input to policy does not establish authority to decide policy.
- Each FR/EN/AR version needs its own approval. Shared factual references are
  reusable; unfinished translations, private notes and unavailable services stay
  outside published output.

Named examples in N1 are source assertions to verify with the teams, not records
to seed into the CMS. Public imagery, figures, partners and outcomes require their
own evidence and rights. S's regulatory references are cited source material;
this analysis does not certify current legal requirements or site compliance.

## Decisions before dependent implementation

Continue using the chosen working structure. Before adopting a proposed structure
change, validate its labels, page positioning, paths and utility scope against
that baseline. For new content modules, collect a small set of confirmed cases,
indicators and capabilities with accountable reviewers. Use those to select
useful modules and define reusable CMS records before drafting and reviewing each
locale. Unadopted alternatives do not block work on the chosen structure.

Contact delivery, applications, registrations, newsletter signup, cookie controls
and optional embeds depend on the actual service and processing arrangements.
The proposal does not override the owner's existing request to retain the footer
newsletter controls in their honest disabled state. Do not change the current
navigation, heroes, footer, CMS schema or service states solely from this analysis.
