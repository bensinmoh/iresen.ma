# Institutional narrative working glossary

These terms accompany the October 2026 PDF alignment. French follows the supplied
reference; English and Arabic are working adaptations for review, not independently
approved source translations. Use the same terminology in headings, body text,
links, accessible names and search metadata.

| French                    | English                            | Arabic                   | Meaning                                                                                |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Recherche & Innovation    | Research & Innovation              | البحث والابتكار          | Core activity                                                                          |
| Développer                | Develop                            | التطوير                  | Produce knowledge; design, adapt or improve solutions                                  |
| Éprouver                  | Test                               | الاختبار                 | Test, compare, evaluate and demonstrate potential; no implied accreditation            |
| Valoriser                 | Valorise                           | التثمين                  | Prepare maturation, transfer or use, beyond patentable technologies                    |
| Études & Expertise        | Studies & Expertise                | الدراسات والخبرة         | Complementary dimension informing choices                                              |
| Compétences & Capacités   | Skills & Capacities                | الكفاءات والقدرات        | Complementary dimension developing people and organisations                            |
| Coopération & Rayonnement | Cooperation & Outreach             | التعاون والإشعاع         | Complementary dimension bringing together partners/resources and sharing contributions |
| Maîtrise technologique    | Technological mastery              | التمكن من التقنيات       | Understand, evaluate, adapt, integrate and evolve technologies                         |
| Maturation                | Maturation                         | الإنضاج                  | Progress toward potential applications, without promising adoption                     |
| Résultat documenté        | Documented result                  | النتيجة الموثقة          | Delivered output or established contribution                                           |

The five master messages cover developing, testing, valorising, informing choices/
strengthening capabilities, and convening partners/amplifying contributions. They
are communication messages, not five departments or five compulsory steps.

The official French mission is reproduced verbatim on Home and Institute.
Adapted headlines never replace it when labelled as the official mission.
